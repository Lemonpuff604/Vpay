The Problem

Most fintech platforms don't give you a bank account.

They give you a wallet inside their account — commingled with every other client. That's platform risk.

  • ✗ If the platform fails, your funds can be frozen
  • ✗ Commingled funds create audit and regulatory complexity
  • ✗ You own a ledger entry, not a bank account
  • ✗ CFOs carry counterparty risk they often don't know exists
Legacy Fintech Model
Fintech Master Account
